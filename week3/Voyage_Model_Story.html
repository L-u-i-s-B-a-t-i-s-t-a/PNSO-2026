Map: Mercator projection, coastlines from Natural Earth (1:50 m); ECA limits drawn approximately from MARPOL Annex VI. The drawn route follows the Dover Strait and the English Channel, crosses the Atlantic on two great circles that keep south of the Grand Banks (as North Atlantic tracks do), and enters the Gulf through the Florida Strait; it places the ship, while each leg keeps the distance and hours of the Task 1 report. Benchmark: Task 1 report (G4), 29-day round trip, 11,186 nm, USD 4.74 M per voyage. Assumptions made here: leg hours scale with 19.6 ÷ speed; main-engine power follows the cube of speed from 20,481 kW at 19.6 kn; port hours, port charges and cargo handling (117.25 h, USD 202 k, USD 2.92 M) are shared among the eight calls in proportion to the benchmark berth fuel; the ECA part of the two Atlantic legs is 1.5 h and 7 h as in the report (or, with the map option, the share of the drawn route inside the ECAs), plus the extra nautical miles you add. Fuel and carbon prices move daily: quote the date and the source when you use them. The defaults are the prices of the Task 1 report; the button “Nov 2026 prices” loads those of the Baseline A model (Rotterdam: VLSFO 706, MGO 1,191 USD/t; EUA € 90.5; 1.0989 USD/EUR). Notation and formulas as in Voyage_Model_A_Basic.py: Tv = Tn + Tp, Qf = SFOC·P·t/106, P = PV(V/V0)n, CRT = OCd·Tv/24 + VC + CHC, VC = FC + PD + TP + CD + ETS. Sources: Ventura, Voyage Model (PNSO 3.01); MARPOL Annex VI; EU ETS; Lecture 2.3b handout.
