Press Play, or click a step. First the flowchart, then Stage 1 (no hull lines yet: only the Design Manual's steps) and Stage 2 (the real hull from Demo 8). Change the numbers and the ship follows.

Read this first. This example is a generalization that illustrates the process. It does not address the peculiarities of a ship whose superstructure is positioned forward, nor the space that must be allocated to the LNG equipment and tanks. Both change the arrangement: where the bridge and the sight line are, how much of the length, the hold and the deck is left for containers. The equations (the allowances, the number of bays, the length, the sight-line check) might then have to be adapted.

Method of the Design Manual (after Alvariño, Azpíroz and Meizoso 1997, section 2.9.3; cross-checked with Papanikolaou 2026), reference ship of the manual, high-cube box 2.438 × 2.896 m. Stage 2 uses the KCS parent of Demo 8 with the Lackenby shift and the stretch, the same function make_hull. Counts are an estimate, not a stowage plan. In steps 4 to 6 the ship outline is generic (the KCS centerline profile stretched to L and D); the hull of make_hull is used from step 7. Omitted for clarity: the tank top, girders, cell guides, the ends of the hatch coamings and the contents of the engine room; their space is inside the 6.55 m bay pitch and the allowances. Validate against the manual's worked example (596 TEU, s_H = 0.45: 14 bays, 7 rows, 4 tiers, L 128.4 m, B 22.05 m, D 11.33 m).
