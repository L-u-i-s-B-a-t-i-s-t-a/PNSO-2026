The cost model step by step, on the general arrangement of the ship. Each step lights up the part being costed, shows its formula with your numbers, and adds the result to the cost tower. Use Next and Back, click a group in the bar under the drawing, or press Play. The example is a 1,300 TEU feeder; change any input on the right.

The cost formulas are those of the Python tool newbuilding_tool_v06 (modules/ and vessels/), run in the browser. Costs in euros; the tool does not state the year of its prices. Engine power in bhp, bores in mm, as in the tool. In the tool the structure weight W is an input; this page can also estimate it with Watson (1998): EN = L(B+T) + 0.85L(D−T) + 0.85Σl·h (superstructures) + 0.75Σl·h (deckhouses), W = K·EN1.36(1 + 0.5(CB′ − 0.70)), K from Papanikolaou, Table 2.24 (container ships 0.036, bulk carriers 0.031, crude oil tankers 0.032), as in the course notebooks. The drawing is generated from L, B, D, T and the engine room and deckhouse inputs; it is a schematic arrangement, not a design. The example inputs (a 1,300 TEU, 157 m feeder with a 11,300 kW two-stroke engine) are this page's. In v06 the nine ships share the same coefficients; the families differ only in the deck and cargo handling group. The last step, from cost to price, is not part of the tool: its percentages are illustrative.
