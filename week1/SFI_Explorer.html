SFI Group System Explorer

The SFI Group System breaks a ship into 8 main groups, about 70 groups and their sub-groups. Shipyards use it to organise specifications, drawings, weights, costs and maintenance. Open it beside the list of contract drawings (Papanikolaou 2026, Table 4.1) to see which part of the ship each drawing describes.
