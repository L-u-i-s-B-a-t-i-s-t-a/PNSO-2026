# Wageningen B-series open-water polynomials

`WageningData.txt` lists the coefficients of the KT (39 terms) and KQ (47 terms) polynomials of the Wageningen B-series propellers, at Rn = 2 × 10^6:

KT = Σ C(s,t,u,v) · J^s · (P/D)^t · (AE/A0)^u · Z^v

The polynomials are from Oosterveld and van Oossanen (1975), as tabulated by Bernitsas, Ray and Kinley (1981), *KT, KQ and Efficiency Curves for the Wageningen B-Series Propellers*, University of Michigan, Report 237.

The file comes unchanged from the MSS toolbox by Thor I. Fossen (https://github.com/cybergalactic/MSS, file `LIBRARY/modeling/utiles/WageningData.txt`). MSS is released under the MIT licence, copied here in `LICENSE`.

Used in homework H2b (Propeller Selection in the Wageningen B-series) and specialization SP04.
