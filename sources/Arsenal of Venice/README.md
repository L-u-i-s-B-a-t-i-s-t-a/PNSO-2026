# Arsenal of Venice

The Arsenal of Venice was the state shipyard of the Venetian Republic. This folder holds a map of it as it stood in 1797, at the fall of the Republic, and the legend of its numbered entries.

| File | What it is | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `Map_Arsenal_of_Venice_1797.jpg` | *Proiezione iconografica del Ces. Reg. Arsenal di Venezia*, the state of the Arsenal's naval forces at the abdication of 12 May 1797 (Disegno II), drawn by Gian Maria Maffioletti and presented to Emperor Francis II by Andrea Querini, 1798 | The map is in the public domain |
| `List_of_Entries_Map_Arsenal_of_Venice.xlsx` | Legend of the map's entries in Italian, English and German, with category, number of entries, approximate area, roofed or open, water or land; sheets for the maps of 1797, 1798 and after Napoleon | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/): Christoph Roser, AllAboutLean.com.
