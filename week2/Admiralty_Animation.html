Objective

Final consequence

Near its parent gives the power to a few per cent. Do not use it across a hump, more than about a knot away, in a hollow with a from another speed, for other proportions, or for large changes of displacement: there the error exceeds 10 %, up to 44 % at 20 kn. Recompute with a resistance method.
