Ship Design Project · 60 %

Two ships for the Azores

Each group designs one of two Baseline ships. The full brief is in 0.2 Ship Design Project (week 1). Groups are formed on Fri 6 Nov. The project is group work done outside class; each group gets a Project Scope Sheet with its deliverables and specializations.

Baseline A

Azores Express

1,300TEU18–19kn1round voyage a week

Container ship, gearless, dual-fuel (LNG and HFO), single shaft, forward superstructure. Lisbon – Ponta Delgada – Praia da Vitória – Lisbon.

Baseline B

Azores Feeder

130TEU11–12kn2island loops a week

Geared container ship, battery-electric with swappable battery containers, two shafts. Hull family of the group's choice; a PSV-type hull is suggested.

Assessment

How the grade is made

60 %Ship Design Project

D1–D10 and two specializations. Due Sun 13 Dec.

20 %Evaluation Test

The design process and the Law and Regulations reading. Fri 27 Nov.

20 %Project presentation

15 minutes per group, then discussion. Fri 18 Dec.

  • Not graded: the weekly quizzes and the homework notebooks. Use them to check yourself.
  • Not tested: ship types, human factors, and stability and freeboard calculations.
  • Software: programming, 3D modelling and drafting are neither taught nor graded. Python (Colab or VS Code) or Excel are both fine.
  • AI tools may be used if you declare where in the report. Every group must be able to explain and reproduce its own work at the presentation.
Bibliography

The textbook

Papanikolaou, A. Ship Design: Methodologies of Preliminary Design. Springer. The e-book is in the Springer Nature collection that the Técnico Library subscribes to: the book on SpringerLink. The lectures cite the 2nd edition (2026).

How to download it through the Técnico Library

  1. Connect to eduroam with your IST account, or to the Técnico VPN: 40-second video.
  2. Open the Library's e-books page. It looks a little different from the video; the Library's news on the collections also leads there.
  3. Choose Springer Nature and search for the book, or open the book on SpringerLink directly.
  4. If Springer asks you to log in, choose Access through your institution, then ULisboa - Instituto Superior Técnico. If it sends you back to the start, check step 1.
  5. Download the book as PDF or EPUB, for your own use. If it does not work, tell the lecturer.

Container ships

Alvariño, R., Azpíroz, J. J. and Meizoso, M. (1997). El proyecto básico del buque mercante. Fondo Editorial de Ingeniería Naval, Colegio de Ingenieros Navales. The method of the container-ship design manual follows its chapter on container ships. Extract: chapter 2.9 Portacontenedores, section 3.8.6 on the number of containers, and the list of symbols (36 pages, in Spanish). For consultation, class use only, do not redistribute.

Hull geometry: KRISO Container Ship (KCS)

KCS hull geometry, Tokyo 2015 Workshop on CFD in Ship Hydrodynamics. National Maritime Research Institute (NMRI), Japan. The bare hull of the KRISO Container Ship, a widely used benchmark container-ship hull: Lpp 230.0 m, B 32.2 m, T 10.8 m, displacement volume 52,030 m³, block coefficient 0.6505, 24 kn (Fn 0.26). IGES file (NURBS surfaces, full scale, metres, one side), table of offsets (CSV, half-breadths at 201 stations and every 0.1 m in height, cut from the IGES) and notes; official download page. Cite the source in your report.
